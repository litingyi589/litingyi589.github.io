Visualization of Transistor Operating Regions and Its Applications

Waveforms reveal voltage changes, but tracing their effects across individual devices can be difficult. My project classifies each MOSFET over time, then highlights where a faulty circuit differs from its healthy counterpart.

3Logic circuit types
4Fault conditions
3MOSFET operating regions

From simulation to insight

  1. Read & prepare

    Load healthy and faulty LTspice .raw files with RawRead. Export time and node-voltage data to CSV.

  2. Classify device states

    Extract Vth0 from NMOS and PMOS model files. Use gate, drain, and source voltages to classify each transistor over time.

  3. Compare & visualize

    Align the time series and display intervals where operating regions differ. Export the resulting comparison images.

Evaluation setup

Transient simulations of a 2-to-4 decoder, a half adder, and a majority circuit, with inputs varying from 0 V to a 1.2 V supply.

Injected fault conditions

  • NMOS threshold voltage increased by 50 mV
  • NMOS channel width reduced by half
  • Supply IR drop modeled with a 50 Ω series resistor
  • Open circuit modeled with a 1 TΩ resistor

Results from my thesis

Original program output for the majority circuit. Each row identifies a transistor in the healthy or faulty circuit; colored segments show intervals with different operating-region classifications.

What the comparison revealed

In the simulated circuits, local device faults tended to produce concentrated differences, while supply degradation produced more widely distributed changes. These patterns can help explore fault influence and propagation through a circuit.

Scope: This is an analysis-support tool, not an automatic fault diagnosis system. Small changes that do not cross operating-region boundaries may be missed. Results depend on simulation models and conditions; application to physical circuits requires further validation.

Source: my undergraduate thesis, “トランジスタの動作領域可視化とその応用,” pp. 1–5. Figures reproduced from p. 3; captions translated and summarized for this portfolio.
